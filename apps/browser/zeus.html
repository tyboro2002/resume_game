Draft: PR Reflections & "Haldis een prijsje" Showcase

Hey Zeus members! As PR for Zeus WPI, I managed both internal and external communication. My responsibilities included delivering presentations to engage students, maintaining the website and social media platforms, designing promotional materials such as graphical posters, and coordinating with event organizers to ensure effective communication. But for this one I did something no one expected.

"Haldis een prijsje" is a dockerized Python/Flask Zeus WPI project that automates menu and price scraping from restaurant websites. By parsing this data into a custom format, it integrates seamlessly with Haldis, an internal ordering system used by Zeus WPI to streamline group food orders. The project automates menu retrieval, reduces manual input and ensures accurate pricing.
